Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 31, 2026

一句话：药点儿不收集、不上传你的任何信息。你的药名、提醒计划和服药记录只保存在你自己的手机上。本应用没有账号系统，正式版本也不申请联网（INTERNET）权限。

1. 这份政策说的是什么

本政策说明药点儿（英文名 Yaodianr，以下简称“本应用”）如何处理你的信息。本应用由 gengxing 开发并发布。

2. 我们处理哪些信息

只有你主动填写的内容，且全部保存在本机：

  • 药品名称
  • 可选的药量说明（你自己输入的自由文本）
  • 可选的备注
  • 提醒时间与重复规则
  • 每次提醒的处理记录：确认服用、稍后提醒或跳过，以及确认时间

除此之外，本应用不读取你的其他信息。

3. 信息存在哪里

上述信息保存在你设备上的应用本地数据库中，只有本应用可以访问。本应用没有服务器，没有账号体系，不提供云同步，也不支持跨设备恢复。

4. 我们不做这些事

  • 不收集设备标识、位置、通讯录、相册、麦克风或相机信息
  • 不上传、不共享、不出售你的用药数据
  • 不把药名或提醒记录用于广告、用户画像或任何第三方分析
  • 当前版本不含广告 SDK，也不含第三方统计或崩溃上报 SDK

5. 应用申请的权限及用途

  • 通知：在你设定的时间发出用药提醒，并在通知里提供确认服用、稍后提醒、跳过这次三个操作
  • 精确闹钟：让提醒尽量准点触发。不授予这项权限时，本应用无法保证按时提醒
  • 开机启动：设备重启后恢复后续提醒
  • 唤醒锁：提醒触发时短暂唤醒设备，以便发出通知

正式发布版本不申请联网（INTERNET）权限，因此本应用在技术上无法把你的数据传到任何地方。发布清单中另有一项 ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE（读取网络连接状态），由系统后台维护组件引入；它只能读取网络是否可用，不能收发任何数据。

6. 系统自动备份

Android 系统可能会把应用数据自动备份到你的个人云账号。是否备份、备份到哪里、恢复后能否在其他设备上使用，由你的系统账号和设置决定，本应用无法控制，也不把这种情况视为本应用的云同步能力。你可以在系统设置中关闭本应用的备份。

7. 你的控制权与数据删除

  • 在应用内「设置」页可以随时执行「清除全部本地数据」，清除后不可恢复
  • 卸载本应用会同时删除本机数据
  • 由于没有账号，不存在需要向服务器申请删除的数据
  • 桌面小组件会在桌面上显示药名，你可以在系统里移除组件，或在应用内开启「锁屏隐藏用药详情」

8. 儿童与未成年人

本应用面向 18 岁及以上的成年人，不面向儿童，也不会主动收集儿童的信息。

9. 政策更新

本政策变更时，我们会更新本页面并修改上方生效日期。若变更涉及信息处理方式的实质调整，我们会在应用内提示。

10. 医疗免责声明

本应用是提醒与记录工具，不是医疗器械，不提供诊断、治疗建议或剂量指导。提醒依赖系统权限和设备状态，可能因权限被关闭、系统限制或厂商后台策略而延迟或失败，请勿把本应用作为唯一的用药保障。用药方案请遵循医生或药师的嘱咐。

11. 联系我们

对本政策有任何疑问，请联系：gengxing@outlook.com。开发者：gengxing。

In one line: Yaodianr does not collect or upload anything. Your medicine names, reminders and dose history stay on your own phone. There is no account system, and the production build does not request the INTERNET permission.

1. What this policy covers

This policy explains how Yaodianr (“the app”) handles your information. The app is developed and published by gengxing.

2. What the app processes

Only what you enter yourself, and all of it is stored on the device:

  • Medicine names
  • An optional dose note (free text you type)
  • An optional note
  • Reminder times and repeat rules
  • The outcome of each reminder: taken, snoozed or skipped, plus the time you confirmed it

The app does not read anything else about you.

3. Where the data is stored

This data is kept in the app's local database on your device, accessible only to the app. There is no server, no account system, no cloud sync and no cross-device restore.

4. What the app never does

  • No collection of device identifiers, location, contacts, photos, microphone or camera
  • No upload, sharing or sale of your medication data
  • No use of medicine names or reminder history for advertising, profiling or third-party analytics
  • No ad SDK in the current version, and no third-party analytics or crash-reporting SDK

5. Permissions and why they are needed

  • Notifications: to alert you at the times you set, with actions to confirm, snooze or skip
  • Exact alarms: so a reminder fires on time. Without it, the app cannot guarantee timely reminders
  • Run at startup: to restore upcoming reminders after the device restarts
  • Wake lock: to briefly wake the device so a reminder notification can be delivered

The production build does not request the INTERNET permission, so it is technically incapable of sending your data anywhere. The release manifest also contains ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE (read network connectivity), pulled in by the system's background maintenance component; it can only read whether a network is available and cannot send or receive any data.

6. System backup

Android may automatically back up app data to your own cloud account. Whether a backup happens, where it is stored, and whether it can be restored on another device are determined by your system account and settings. The app does not control this and does not treat it as a cloud sync feature. You can turn off backup for this app in system settings.

7. Your control and deleting data

  • “Clear all local data” in Settings removes everything at any time; this cannot be undone
  • Uninstalling the app deletes the data on the device
  • Because there is no account, there is no server-side data to request deletion of
  • The home screen widget shows medicine names on your home screen; you can remove the widget, or enable “Hide medication details on the lock screen” in the app

8. Children

The app is intended for adults aged 18 and over. It is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect information from children.

9. Changes to this policy

When this policy changes we will update this page and the effective date above. If a change materially affects how information is handled, we will tell you inside the app.

10. Medical disclaimer

The app is a reminder and record-keeping tool. It is not a medical device and does not provide diagnosis, treatment or dosing advice. Reminders depend on system permissions and device state, and may be delayed or fail because of disabled permissions, system limits or manufacturer background restrictions. Please do not rely on this app as your only safeguard for taking medication. Follow the instructions of your doctor or pharmacist.
